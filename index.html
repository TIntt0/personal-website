About

Welcome to my homepage! I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Chemistry, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, working under the supervision of Prof. Xinglong Zhang. Our group develops computational chemistry tools and machine-learning potentials to automate workflows and accelerate chemical discovery.

Before joining CUHK in Aug 2026, I obtained my Bachelor of Science in Chemistry at Zhejiang University.

Research Interests

DFT-based mechanistic study of organic/organometallic catalysis, development of MLIPs for complex chemical systems, and automated high-throughput quantum-chemistry workflows integrated with machine learning.
